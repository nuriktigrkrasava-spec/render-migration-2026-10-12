Saya Courier: location during an active shift

Google Play review demonstration for com.wiregroup.sayacourier, version 0.1.1 (2).

The unmodified signed release APK runs on an Android 11 (API 30) emulator against an isolated test backend. The courier, branch, PIN and coordinates are synthetic. No real customer or employee data is shown.

Open the MP4 directly

The recording shows sign-in, the location disclosure, user-initiated shift start, the Android location permission, the foreground notification, operation while the app is in the background, and user-initiated shift end. GPS locations are supplied by the emulator for this demonstration.

This is a feature demonstration, not a production account or a claim that every Android version was tested. Location is collected only during a shift explicitly started by the courier. The courier can stop the service from the app or its persistent notification.
